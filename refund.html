Refund and Cancellation Policy

Business: ARORA.HOST

This policy explains how customers can request cancellation or a refund for hosting services.

1. Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation by emailing hello@arora.host. Please include your registered email address and order details.

2. Refund Requests

Customers may request a refund within 7 days of their initial hosting purchase. Each request will be reviewed based on the service provided and applicable law.

3. Non-Refundable Items

Domain registration, renewal, transfer fees, and third-party services may be non-refundable once processed, where permitted by law. Any such exclusions will be communicated before purchase.

4. Approved Refunds

Approved refunds will be returned through the original payment method where possible. Processing times depend on the payment provider and bank.

5. Service Issues

If you experience a billing error or service issue, contact us so we can review the matter.

6. Contact Us

Email: hello@arora.host
